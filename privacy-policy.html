Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 23, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how David Imm ("we," "us," or "our") handles information in connection with the following add-ins for Autodesk Revit (each an "App," together the "Apps") and related services:

  • BIM Pro
  • 3D Column Grid Marks (shown in Revit as the COL MARK tab)

1. Information We Collect

The Apps are desktop add-ins for Autodesk Revit and are designed to operate primarily within the user's local Revit environment. We do not sell personal information and do not use information from Revit project files for advertising. The Apps do not include analytics, tracking, or advertising code.

Depending on how an App is licensed and used, information that may be provided to us or our service providers can include information necessary to identify and manage a user's subscription or entitlement, such as name, email address, Autodesk App Store account information, subscription status, and technical information required to validate a license.

2. License (Entitlement) Check

To confirm that a user has an active trial or subscription, each App sends the Autodesk user ID of the account signed in to Revit, together with the App's identifier, to Autodesk's App Store entitlement service (apps.autodesk.com). Autodesk replies whether the account is entitled to use the App. This request goes directly to Autodesk; it is not sent to us. No Revit project data is included.

The result of the check is written to a log file on the user's own computer to help diagnose licensing problems. This log can contain the Autodesk user ID and is not sent to us unless the user chooses to share it for support.

3. Information Stored on Your Computer

The Apps store settings and workflow information locally on the user's computer. This information is used only to provide the Apps' functionality and is not collected by us.

AppStored locallyLocation
BIM Pro Saved sessions, workset preferences, export settings, licensing log Folders named BIM Pro / BIM_Pro under the user's AppData
3D Column Grid Marks Grid Marks options, Auto Update on/off, marker family location, licensing log %LOCALAPPDATA%\ColMark3D

4. How We Use Information

Information we receive is used only as reasonably necessary to:

  • Provide, activate, and manage App subscriptions and trials;
  • Verify user entitlement to use the Apps;
  • Provide customer support and respond to requests;
  • Maintain security and prevent unauthorized use;
  • Diagnose technical problems when information is voluntarily provided by a user; and
  • Meet legal and contractual obligations.

5. Revit Project Data

The Apps are not intended to collect or transmit the contents of a user's Revit models, drawings, designs, or other project files to us. Changes the Apps make (for example, markers placed by 3D Column Grid Marks) are saved only in the user's own Revit project.

Users should not send project files or other confidential information to us unless necessary for support and specifically requested or voluntarily provided by the user.

6. Third-Party Services

The Apps rely on the Autodesk App Store and third-party services for subscription, entitlement, payment, hosting, or related functions. Information provided to those services is handled according to their respective privacy policies and terms, including the Autodesk Privacy Statement.

Third-party service providers used by the Apps are expected to protect personal information consistent with applicable contractual and legal requirements.

7. Data Retention

We retain personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the Apps, administer subscriptions, provide support, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce agreements.

Local settings, workflow information, and licensing logs remain on the user's computer until the user deletes them. Users can remove them at any time by deleting the folders listed in Section 3.

10. Security

11. Children's Privacy
