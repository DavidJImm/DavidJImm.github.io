BIM Pro Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 7, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how BIM Pro ("BIM Pro," "we," "us," or "our") handles information in connection with the BIM Pro add-in for Autodesk Revit and related services.

1. Information We Collect

BIM Pro is a desktop add-in for Autodesk Revit. BIM Pro is designed to operate primarily within the user's local Revit environment. We do not sell personal information and do not use information from Revit project files for advertising.

Depending on how BIM Pro is licensed and used, information that may be provided to us or our service providers can include information necessary to identify and manage a user's subscription or entitlement, such as name, email address, Autodesk Marketplace account information, subscription status, and technical information required to validate a license.

BIM Pro may also store application settings and workflow information locally on the user's computer, such as saved session and workset preferences. This information is used to provide BIM Pro's functionality and is not collected by us simply because it exists in a Revit project.

2. How We Use Information

Information we receive is used only as reasonably necessary to:

  • Provide, activate, and manage BIM Pro subscriptions and trials;
  • Verify user entitlement to use BIM Pro;
  • Provide customer support and respond to requests;
  • Maintain security and prevent unauthorized use;
  • Diagnose technical problems when information is voluntarily provided by a user; and
  • Meet legal and contractual obligations.

3. Revit Project Data

BIM Pro is not intended to collect or transmit the contents of a user's Revit models, drawings, designs, or other project files to us. BIM Pro's local workflow features operate on the user's computer.

Users should not send project files or other confidential information to us unless necessary for support and specifically requested or voluntarily provided by the user.

4. Third-Party Services

BIM Pro may rely on Autodesk Marketplace and third-party services for subscription, entitlement, payment, hosting, or related functions. Information provided to those services is handled according to their respective privacy policies and terms.

Third-party service providers used by BIM Pro are expected to protect personal information consistent with applicable contractual and legal requirements.

5. Data Retention

We retain personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide BIM Pro, administer subscriptions, provide support, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce agreements.

Local BIM Pro settings and workflow information remain on the user's computer unless the user removes them or they are removed as part of uninstalling or resetting BIM Pro.

6. Data Deletion and User Requests

Users may request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information held by us, subject to applicable law and legitimate business or legal requirements.

To request deletion or ask questions about personal information, contact us using the email address below. We will review and respond to requests within a reasonable period.

7. Revoking Consent

Where processing is based on consent, users may withdraw that consent by contacting us. Withdrawing consent does not affect processing that was lawful before consent was withdrawn or processing that is permitted or required by law.

8. Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational measures to protect personal information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. No method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

9. Children's Privacy

BIM Pro is a professional software product and is not directed toward children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when our practices or legal requirements change. The effective date at the top of this page indicates when the policy was most recently updated.
