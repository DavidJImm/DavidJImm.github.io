BIM Pro Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 1, 2026

BIM Pro is a productivity add-in for Autodesk Revit. This Privacy Policy explains what information BIM Pro may collect or process, how that information is used, and how users can request information or deletion.

1. Information BIM Pro Collects

BIM Pro is designed to operate primarily within the user's local Autodesk Revit environment. BIM Pro does not intentionally collect, upload, or transmit the contents of a user's Revit models, drawings, project files, or other project data to the BIM Pro publisher.

BIM Pro may store configuration information locally on the user's computer. This may include application settings, saved BIM Pro preferences, workset/session information, and information necessary for the operation of BIM Pro.

When BIM Pro uses Autodesk Marketplace subscription or entitlement services, information necessary to verify the user's BIM Pro subscription or entitlement may be exchanged with Autodesk or its applicable services. BIM Pro does not use this information for advertising or sell it to third parties.

2. Revit Project Data

BIM Pro operates on information within Autodesk Revit when the user invokes a BIM Pro function. Depending on the function being used, BIM Pro may read or modify Revit project information, schedules, worksets, sheets, elements, coordinates, or other information necessary to perform the requested operation.

This information is processed for the purpose of performing the requested BIM Pro operation. BIM Pro does not intentionally transmit the contents of Revit project files to the BIM Pro publisher.

3. Excel and Other Files

Certain BIM Pro features allow users to exchange information between Autodesk Revit and Microsoft Excel or export information to PDF, DWG, or other files.

These files are processed on the user's computer as part of the requested operation. BIM Pro does not intentionally upload these files or their contents to the BIM Pro publisher.

4. How Information Is Used

Information processed by BIM Pro is used to:

  • Provide BIM Pro functionality requested by the user.
  • Store application preferences and configuration information.
  • Maintain the user's BIM Pro working environment.
  • Verify applicable BIM Pro subscription or entitlement status.
  • Provide technical support when information is voluntarily supplied by the user.

5. Third-Party Services

BIM Pro may rely on Autodesk services for Marketplace subscription and entitlement verification. Autodesk's handling of information is governed by Autodesk's applicable privacy policies and terms.

BIM Pro does not sell personal information to third parties and does not use personal information for third-party advertising.

6. Data Retention

BIM Pro's locally stored application and configuration information remains on the user's computer until it is removed by the user, removed during an applicable uninstall process, or otherwise overwritten or deleted as part of normal application operation.

Information handled by Autodesk in connection with Marketplace purchases or subscription entitlement is retained and handled according to Autodesk's applicable policies.

7. Data Deletion and User Requests

Users may remove BIM Pro's locally stored application information by uninstalling BIM Pro and deleting remaining BIM Pro application data from their computer, where applicable.

Users may request information concerning personal data associated with BIM Pro, or request deletion where applicable, by contacting the publisher using the contact information below.

Requests relating to Autodesk Marketplace accounts, purchases, or Autodesk-managed subscription information may need to be directed to Autodesk.

8. Security

BIM Pro is designed to minimize the collection and transmission of user information. Users should also maintain appropriate security practices for their computers, Revit projects, Autodesk accounts, and other software used with BIM Pro.

9. Children's Privacy

BIM Pro is professional software intended for use with Autodesk Revit and is not directed toward children.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time to reflect changes to BIM Pro, applicable services, or legal requirements. The effective date shown at the beginning of this policy will be updated when material changes are made.
